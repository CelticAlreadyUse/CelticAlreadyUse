## About me

I'm **Kanisius Wahyu**, a full-stack developer and **Tech Lead at Mantik Teknologi Nusantara**, currently studying **Informatics at Sanata Dharma University**.

I enjoy working where **product thinking, user experience, and software architecture** meet. Beyond writing code, I'm interested in understanding business processes, making sound technical trade-offs, and helping teams turn complicated requirements into maintainable systems.

My current interests include:

- **Enterprise application engineering** — modular systems, integration boundaries, and maintainable architectures.
- **Aviation operations software** — exploring workflows across flight operations, maintenance, and inventory.
- **Offline-first applications** — reliable experiences in environments with intermittent connectivity.
- **Security & platform foundations** — identity, authorization, auditability, and dependable integrations.
- **Developer experience & design systems** — building consistent, usable interfaces and effective engineering workflows.

> I care about more than making software work. I care about making it useful, understandable, and sustainable.

## What I'm working on

**At Mantik**, I contribute to technical direction, architecture decisions, and early-stage product development for enterprise-focused software. One area of exploration is an aviation operations platform connecting operational workflows with maintenance and inventory processes.

I'm also continuing to learn and experiment with **micro-frontends, Module Federation, modern frontend architecture, 3D web experiences, and engineering practices**.
